# Community standards

SYMTRI welcomes people of every background and experience level. Participation in this repository's issues, pull requests, and other project spaces means following these standards.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific. Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Help newcomers understand the project and give feedback they can act on.
- Respect differing experiences, accessibility needs, and perspectives.
- Credit others' work and respect privacy, source attribution, and licensing.
- Accept corrections and focus on making the shared work better.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, doxxing, and publishing someone's private information without consent are unacceptable. So are repeated disruptive comments, spam, and retaliation against someone who raises a concern.

## Reporting concerns

For a non-sensitive moderation concern, contact the maintainer [@stanleycyang](https://github.com/stanleycyang) in the relevant project conversation. Do not publish private information or sensitive details in an issue.

For abuse requiring a private platform report, use GitHub's **Report abuse** control on the relevant user or content. These reports go to GitHub, rather than to the project maintainer. Security vulnerabilities have a separate [private reporting route](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, request a change in behavior, close disruptive conversations, or restrict participation temporarily or permanently. Responses should consider severity, repetition, and the impact on affected people. Threats, harassment, and serious privacy violations can warrant immediate restrictions.

These standards apply in project spaces and when someone represents SYMTRI publicly. They apply to maintainers and contributors alike.
